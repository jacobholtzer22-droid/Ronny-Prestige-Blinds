# Questions for Ronny before launch

One line per open question. Nothing here has been guessed at on the site: where
an answer is missing, the matching field is null and the block does not render.

## Photos

- Send original job photos, straight off the phone or camera, at full resolution. Everything on the site right now was pulled off the current website, where it had already been compressed by WordPress.
- Do you have photos of a drapery job and a blinds job in a finished room? Those two are the thinnest.
- Do you have photos of a commercial or hospitality job we are allowed to show?

## Brand and identity

- Do you have the logo as a transparent PNG or an SVG? The versions on the current site are small and have a white background baked in.
- How do you want your name shown on the site, if at all? Right now the site never names you.

## The business

- How many years should the site say? Your intake answer was twenty; the current website says "more than 10 years" in several places and "Over 15 Years Experience" in one.
- Which products can actually be installed the same day, and which ones cannot?
- Is the Davie address still a working location, and do you want it published? The site currently shows Coral Springs only, and lists Davie as a service area.
- Do you carry any insurance you would like named on the site?
- Is there a warranty on the products or the installation, and how long is it?

## The offer

- "10 shades 1 free": is that ten paid and an eleventh free, or one of the ten free?
- Does it cover blinds and shutters too, or shades only?
- Are there any exclusions, a minimum order, or an end date?

## Products

- Do you carry faux wood blinds? The current website names wood, metal, and vinyl only.
- Do you carry cellular or honeycomb shades? One of your reviews mentions honeycomb shades, but no page on the current site sells them.
- Can shades a customer already owns be converted to motorized, or does that always mean new shades?
- How long does a charge last on a battery motor?
- Are the outdoor patio shades rated for a particular wind speed?
- Is blackout lining available on drapery?
- Roughly how long does a shutter order take from measuring to installation?

## Brands

- Are you an authorized dealer for Hunter Douglas or Somfy? Both show up in your Instagram captions, so neither is named on the site.
- Besides Norman and Lafayette, is there any brand you want named?

## Listings and reviews

- You have two Google listings, one for Coral Springs and one for Davie. Which is verified, and which should the site link to?
- Which Facebook page is current? The site links prestigeblind100; your old page also tagged PrestigeWBlinds.
- Are the five testimonials on your current site real reviews we can keep showing?
- Can you send the direct Google review link? The current site uses a third party shortlink, which is not linked from the new site.
- Do you want your Angi listing linked? It is not on the site today.

## Website structure

- The old site had a separate page for roller, Roman, sheer, and outdoor patio shades. The new site folds all four into one Shades page. Do you want them split back out?
