## What we see in Coconut Creek homes

The city is organized largely into planned villages and is known for its tree canopy, which genuinely changes the brief. Streets with real shade get softer, more dappled light, so the heaviest solar fabric is usually more than the room needs and a light filtering shade or a shutter reads better. The housing runs from single-family streets to townhomes, villas, and large 55+ communities. In a townhome or villa the window sizes repeat, which means one material and color across the whole unit looks deliberate and usually installs in a single visit. In the 55+ communities, cordless and motorized options come up constantly, because they take both the reaching and the cords out of daily life.
