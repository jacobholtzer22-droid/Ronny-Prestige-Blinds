## Window treatments in {area.name}

{area.name} is built around its waterways, and a large share of its houses back onto a canal or the Intracoastal. That means water reflecting light back up into rooms on top of the direct sun, in houses where the whole rear wall is often glass because the view is the reason the house exists. Downtown and along the beach, the high rise condos have the same problem in a different package.

## What that means for the windows

Nobody on a canal wants their view covered, so solar screen shades do most of the work here: glare and heat off, water still visible. In the older neighborhoods such as Victoria Park and Rio Vista, the architecture suits plantation shutters, and the smaller original windows take them well. In a condo, motorized shades are often the practical answer on a wall of glass with no easy access.
