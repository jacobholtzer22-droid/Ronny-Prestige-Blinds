## What we see in Fort Lauderdale homes

A large share of the city backs onto a canal or the Intracoastal, which means light bouncing off the water on top of direct sun, in houses where the entire rear wall is glass because the water is the reason the house exists. Solar screen shades are the usual answer there: glare and heat down, view kept. Downtown and along the beach, the high-rise condos have tall glass with no easy access, which is motorized territory. In the older neighborhoods such as Victoria Park and Rio Vista, the original windows are smaller and better proportioned, and plantation shutters suit the period of the houses rather than fighting it.
