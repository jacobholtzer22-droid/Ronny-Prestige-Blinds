## What we see in Deerfield Beach homes

The city mixes beachfront condo buildings, long-established single-family streets, and large planned communities, so one order can run from a studio balcony slider to a full house of windows. On the beachfront units the brief is almost always the same: solar screen for the day so the view survives, and a blackout layer for the bedrooms so the sunrise does not decide when you get up. A double roller does both on one window. Further inland the housing is older and the openings are smaller and more numerous, which suits horizontal blinds and shutters. Sliding glass doors onto a balcony are the single most common opening in the city, and verticals, panel track shutters, and wide zebra shades all leave the doorway usable.
