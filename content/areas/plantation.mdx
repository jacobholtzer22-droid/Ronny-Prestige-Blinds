## What that means for the windows

A mature canopy changes the brief. Rooms that are shaded for part of the day do not need the heaviest solar fabric, and light filtering shades or plantation shutters often read better, because the aim is softening dappled light rather than fighting a wall of it. Houses of that era also tend to have smaller, more numerous windows than new construction, which suits shutters, and awkward original sizes, which is why every opening gets measured rather than assumed.
