## What we see in Plantation homes

The city has the tree canopy to prove its age, and the shade changes what a room actually needs. Where mature trees filter the light already, the job is softening dappled light rather than fighting a wall of it, so light filtering shades and plantation shutters usually read better than the heaviest solar fabric. Much of the housing is single-story from that era, with smaller and more numerous windows, which suits shutters and horizontal blinds. Newer gated communities and the larger lots out in Plantation Acres are a different proposition again, with bigger rear glass and wide sliders that want panel track shutters or a wide shade. Carrying one material across mismatched openings is what makes a house done in stages look finished.
