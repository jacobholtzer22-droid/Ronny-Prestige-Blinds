## What we see in Boca Raton homes

The city splits into three different window problems. The oceanfront condo towers are mostly east-facing glass, where sunrise comes straight into the living room and nobody wants to give up the water view, so solar screens and zebra shades do most of the work. The country club communities and older single-family neighborhoods inland have the window proportions that suit shutters, with arches and deep reveals. And the newer subdivisions west of the interstate have the taller glass and wider sliders that come with recent construction. Associations here often specify what a window shows from the outside, which is simple to meet and worth settling before anything is ordered.
