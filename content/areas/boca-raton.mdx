## Window treatments in {area.name}

{area.name} runs from the ocean back through country club communities to newer subdivisions west of the interstate, and the window problem changes as you move across it. Condo towers near the water are mostly glass facing east, with sunrise straight into the living room and a view nobody wants to give up. Inland, the single family neighborhoods have more conventional windows but far more of them.

## What that means for the windows

On the barrier island and the oceanfront buildings, solar screen shades are usually the starting point: they take the glare and the heat off without taking the water away. Where privacy matters more than view, zebra shades do both in one shade. Inland, plantation shutters remain the most common request, and they suit the architecture of the older neighborhoods well.
