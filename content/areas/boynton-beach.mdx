Boynton Beach sits on the Palm Beach County coast between Delray Beach and Lake Worth, with Intracoastal and beachfront property to the east and a large number of planned and 55+ communities west of the interstate.

Boynton Beach is at the northern edge of our service area in Palm Beach County and still a normal run for us. A large share of the work here is in 55+ and planned communities, where association rules and a strong preference for cordless operation shape most of the order. Both are easy to plan for once they are on the table at the consultation, which is free as it is everywhere we work.

## What we see in Boynton Beach homes

The city divides at the interstate. East of it you have Intracoastal and beachfront property, where light reflecting off the water adds to the direct sun and solar screen shades are the usual starting point, with a blackout layer behind them for the bedrooms. West of it are the large planned and 55+ communities, where the housing is more uniform, window sizes repeat, and whole-house orders are common. Cordless and motorized options come up constantly in those communities for the obvious reason: they remove the reaching and the cords entirely. Where a community has settled on a look, we can usually match the material and color, and where an association specifies the backing we meet that requirement.
