## Window treatments in {area.name}

{area.name} sits on the coast north of Fort Lauderdale, with oceanfront condo buildings along the beach, older single family neighborhoods behind them, and a good deal of recent redevelopment in between. The closer a property sits to the water, the harder the conditions on anything hung in a window: salt in the air, direct eastern sun, and a lot of it.

## What that means for the windows

Material choice matters more here than almost anywhere else on this list. Vinyl and composite shutters hold up where wood would rather not, and they wipe clean, which counts on a balcony slider that gets opened every day. For the east facing glass, solar screen shades take the morning glare off without closing the room in. Blackout roller shades are the usual request in bedrooms facing the sunrise.
