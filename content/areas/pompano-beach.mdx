## What we see in Pompano Beach homes

The city runs from oceanfront condo buildings along the beach, back through older single-family neighborhoods, with a good deal of recent redevelopment in between. The closer a property sits to the water, the harder the conditions: salt in the air, direct eastern sun, and plenty of it. Vinyl and composite shutters hold up where wood would rather not, and they wipe clean, which counts on a balcony slider that gets opened every day. East-facing bedrooms are the other recurring job, and blackout roller shades are the only thing that genuinely stops a sunrise. Behind the beach, the older streets have smaller, more numerous windows that suit shutters and horizontal blinds, almost none of them a standard size.
