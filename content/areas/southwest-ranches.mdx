## What we see in Southwest Ranches homes

The town is rural-residential by design, with large minimum lot sizes, no sidewalks or streetlights, and an equestrian character it has deliberately protected. The houses are mostly large custom builds on acreage, which means big openings, tall glass, and very few standard window sizes. Two things follow. Anything high needs motorizing, because on a custom house the tall glass is usually the feature and it is never reachable. And privacy is less of a constraint than it is in a subdivision, because there is no neighbor twenty feet away, so you can often use a lighter and more open fabric and keep the view that the acreage bought you in the first place.
