Sunrise is close to our Coral Springs base and quick to schedule. A good share of the work here is in 55+ communities and townhome developments, where window sizes repeat within a development and a whole-unit order is usually the cleanest way to do it. The in-home consultation and the estimate are free, and same-day estimates are standard.

## What we see in Sunrise homes

Much of Sunrise was built out through the 1970s and 1980s, with single-family neighborhoods, townhome developments, large 55+ communities, and newer construction toward the western edge. The older housing has the window proportions of its era: smaller openings, more of them, and sliding doors onto a screened patio. For those sliders, verticals or a wide zebra shade keep the doorway usable, and where the sun on the patio is the real problem rather than the privacy, outdoor screen shades on the patio itself are the better answer. In the 55+ communities, cordless and motorized options come up in almost every conversation, because they remove both the reaching and the cords, and we fit and program the motors as part of the job.
