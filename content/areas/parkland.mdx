Parkland sits at the northern edge of Broward County and its zoning shows: large lots, low density, equestrian trails, and newer gated subdivisions rather than apartment blocks. Houses built to that scale come with two-story entries, tall front windows, and wide sliding doors onto the yard.

## Why Parkland homeowners choose {config.displayName}

Parkland is fifteen minutes from our Coral Springs office, which is the practical reason most of the work here goes smoothly: measuring, delivery, and installation are all a short run rather than a half-day each. The houses are large, so the orders tend to be whole-house rather than a room at a time, and that suits the way we work. One measure across every opening, one delivery, one installation day, and the same material and color carried through the house so it reads as a single decision.

## What we see in Parkland homes

The housing stock is newer and built to a consistent standard: gated subdivisions, generous lots, two-story entries, and a lot of rear glass facing the yard or the pool. Two things follow from that. The tall windows above the door and the staircase are the ones worth motorizing, because nobody is going to handle them by hand twice a day. And the wide sliding doors onto the yard need something that stacks clear of the opening rather than crowding it, which in practice means panel track shutters, verticals, or a wide zebra shade. Most Parkland communities also review anything visible from the street, so we settle the frame and color before the order goes in.
