## What we see in Pembroke Pines homes

The two halves of the city need different things. The older eastern neighborhoods have smaller, more numerous windows and the odd sizes that come with housing built decades ago, which suits shutters and horizontal blinds and means everything gets measured. The newer master-planned and 55+ communities in the west bring taller windows, wider sliding doors, and open-plan rooms with a lot of glass, which is motorized-shade and panel-track territory. Several of the large communities specify how a window reads from the street, and we settle that requirement before ordering. There is no minimum order, either: plenty of jobs here start as one room that was unbearable in the afternoon and grow once the difference is obvious.
