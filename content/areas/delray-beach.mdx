## Window treatments in {area.name}

{area.name} covers more ground architecturally than its size suggests. There are historic bungalows in the neighborhoods either side of Atlantic Avenue, beachfront condos east of the road, and country club communities spreading west, and a window treatment that suits one of those looks wrong in the next.

## What that means for the windows

In the older bungalows, the windows are smaller, the trim has character worth keeping, and custom drapery or a traditional tilt shutter suits the period rather than fighting it. The beachfront condos are the opposite brief: wide glass, hard light, and a view to protect, which is solar screen or zebra shade territory. Out west, the country club houses have the tall windows and wide sliders that motorized shades were made for.
