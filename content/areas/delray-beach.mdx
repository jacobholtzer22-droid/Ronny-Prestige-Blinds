Delray Beach covers more ground architecturally than its size suggests. There are historic bungalows in the neighborhoods either side of Atlantic Avenue, beachfront condos east of the road, and country club communities spreading west, and a treatment that suits one looks wrong in the next.

Delray Beach is in southern Palm Beach County, inside our fifty-mile radius, and we come up regularly. The reason the consultation matters more than usual here is that the city changes character over very short distances: a treatment that is right for a bungalow two streets off Atlantic Avenue looks wrong in a beachfront condo half a mile away. ## What we see in Delray Beach homes

There are three distinct kinds of property. The historic bungalows in the neighborhoods either side of Atlantic Avenue have smaller windows and trim worth keeping, and custom drapery or a traditional tilt shutter suits the period rather than arguing with it. The beachfront condos east of the road are the opposite brief: wide glass, hard light, and a view that is the whole reason for the unit, which is solar screen and zebra shade territory. West of town, the country club communities have the tall windows and wide sliders that motorized shades were built for. One city, three different answers.
