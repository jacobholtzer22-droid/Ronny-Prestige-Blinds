## What we see in Weston homes

The city is master planned and most of it went up from the 1980s onward, close to the Everglades and with very little between the houses and the western horizon. That is the whole story for window treatments here: late afternoon sun arrives low and hard across open ground at exactly the hour people get home, so west-facing rooms heat up and glare badly. Solar screens and room darkening rollers are the standard answer. The other recurring feature is the two-story great room, which is where motorized shades stop being a luxury and become the only practical way to reach the glass. Several communities specify how a window reads from outside, and that is settled before anything is ordered.
