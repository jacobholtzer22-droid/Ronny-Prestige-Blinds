## Window treatments in {area.name}

{area.name} is a master planned city on the western edge of Broward County, close to the Everglades, and most of it went up from the 1980s onward in planned communities. That gives it something most cities here do not have: consistency. Houses within a community often share a handful of floor plans, which means the same window sizes and the same sun exposure turn up again and again.

## What that means for the windows

The western exposure is the thing. Late afternoon sun comes in low and hard across open ground with little to break it, so west facing rooms heat up and glare badly at exactly the hour people are home. Solar screens and room darkening roller shades handle that. Two story great rooms are common in these floor plans, which is where motorized shades stop being a luxury and start being the only practical way to reach the glass.
