## Window treatments in {area.name}

{area.name} has kept its western character in a way the rest of Broward County has not. Horse trails, acreage, and ranch properties sit next to newer subdivisions and several college campuses, so the housing stock runs from older single story homes to recent builds, often within a few streets of each other.

## What that means for the windows

Older ranch homes tend to have wide, low windows and sliding doors that were never covered well in the first place, and they are the kind of opening where custom sizing matters more than style. Newer subdivision houses come with the tall entry windows and big sliders that are standard now. Both are measured on site rather than estimated, because very little of this housing stock is a standard size.
