## What we see in Davie homes

The town has kept its western character deliberately: horse trails, acreage, ranch properties, and large lots sitting next to newer subdivisions and several college campuses. Older ranch houses tend to have wide, low windows and sliding doors that were never covered well in the first place, and very few of them are a standard size. Newer subdivision houses in the same town come with the tall entry windows and big sliders that are standard in recent construction. Where a house has been added to over the years, keeping one material and one color across mismatched openings is what makes the result look deliberate rather than accidental, and that is a decision we make across the whole order.
