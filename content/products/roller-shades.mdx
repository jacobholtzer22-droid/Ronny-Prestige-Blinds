## What is a roller shade?

A single panel of material on a tube, raised by cord, chain, or motor. It is the simplest thing you can put on a window and very often the right one: nothing to dust between slats, nothing to tangle, and a clean line when it is up. Because the fabric does all the work, the decision is which fabric rather than which mechanism.

## Choosing the fabric

Vinyl is durable, cost-effective, wipes clean, and resists moisture, which makes it the sensible answer in a kitchen, a bathroom, or anywhere humid. Light filtering fabric is semi-transparent: it turns hard direct sun into a soft, even glow, which suits living rooms, dining areas, and kitchens. Room darkening fabric takes the light down further for a cozier, more private room. Blackout fabric is heavy and opaque and shuts the light out, which is what a bedroom or a media room wants.

## Solar screens and double rollers

A solar screen is an open-weave fabric that cuts glare and heat while you still see straight through it. On a wall of glass facing the afternoon sun it is usually the first thing we suggest, because it solves the problem without taking away the reason the window is there. Where one shade cannot do both jobs, a double roller carries a solar screen and a blackout shade on the same window: screen through the day, blackout at night.

## Operation

Cord, chain, or motor. On a tall window, a wide span, or anything behind furniture, motorizing a roller is the single easiest upgrade to live with, and our team fits and programs it as part of the installation.
