## What a sheer shade does

The fabric diffuses daylight. Direct sun arrives as an even wash instead of a hard edge, the room stays bright, and you can still see out through the shade. It is the treatment for a window with a view worth keeping, in a room that is already well lit and does not need closing down.

## Privacy, honestly

A sheer shade softens what anyone outside can see rather than stopping it, and it does more at night than during the day, when the light balance works against you. If privacy is the main thing you are solving, we will say so and point you at zebra shades or a second layer instead. Pairing a sheer with a roller behind it is a common answer in a bedroom: sheer by day, something solid after dark.

## Colors and rooms

They come in a range of colors and patterns chosen to sit with a room rather than announce themselves, which is why they turn up most often in living rooms and bedrooms that have already been decorated. We bring samples to the consultation so you can hold the fabric against your own wall color and flooring, in your own light, before anything is ordered.

## Operation

Sheer shades are light, so the hardware is straightforward and they can be motorized without fuss. On a tall window that saves the reaching, and we set the open and closed limits and program the control before we leave.
