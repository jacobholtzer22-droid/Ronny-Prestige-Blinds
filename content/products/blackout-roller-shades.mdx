## What blackout fabric actually does

The material itself passes no light. That is the difference between blackout and room darkening, which cuts most of the light but keeps a dim glow. If you want a bedroom genuinely dark at two in the afternoon, or a media room where the screen is the brightest thing in it, blackout is the fabric that gets you there.

## Getting the edges right

No roller shade is a perfect seal, because the fabric has to clear the brackets to roll. What decides how dark the room really gets is the mounting: fitted outside the frame with a generous overlap, the light that would otherwise creep down the sides has nowhere to go. That is a measuring and installation decision, not a fabric one, which is why we measure on site rather than quote from a size you read off a tape.

## Where they earn their keep here

Bedrooms facing the sunrise, which in South Florida means early and bright. Nurseries and any room where somebody sleeps during the day. Media rooms. In this climate the heat arrives with the light, so a blackout shade on an east or west window also takes a noticeable load off the air conditioning through the worst of the day.

## Pairing and operation

Where you want the view through the day and full dark at night, a double roller puts a solar screen in front of the blackout shade on the same window. Blackout rollers can also be motorized, which is worth doing in a bedroom: you can run them from the bedside or set them to close on a schedule.
