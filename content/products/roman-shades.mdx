## The six folds

Flat gives a clean, neat stack with a straight bottom edge, which is the most popular and the most contemporary. Cascade is the classic: fabric pleats that gather as the shade moves, for a waterfall look. Pleated has sewn-in pleats every few inches, so it reads like an accordion. Aventura is close to cascade without the bulk, with a precise horizontal line sewn into the fabric. Relaxed drapes slightly in the middle with straight sides, which adds a little softness to a plain room. Tulip is the closest to the original: a soft drape with gathered sides.

## Fabric decides everything

Cotton, linen, silk, or polyester, in a wide range of colors, patterns, and textures. The fabric is what makes a Roman shade hang the way it does, so two shades in the same style and different cloth are not the same shade. That is the main reason we bring samples to the house rather than asking you to choose from a screen.

## Light and heat

Most styles are available in solar and UV filtering fabric, and even the ones that are not give you better temperature control than bare glass, because you have added a layer. For a room you want properly dark, a lining or a blackout roller behind the Roman is the combination that works, and the Roman keeps doing the decorative job.

## Operation

Roman shades can be motorized, which lifts the shade on a hidden tube rather than a cord. On a tall window that is a practical gain, and in a house with small children or pets removing the cord removes a hazard.
