## What they solve

Five things at once: UV, glare, heat, weather, and bugs. The screen fabric shields you from direct sun and takes the harsh light down, it keeps the covered area cooler by absorbing heat before it reaches you, it holds off gusts and rain across the opening, and it puts a barrier between you and the mosquitoes that arrive with the humid months. It also protects outdoor furniture, which otherwise fades and wears out faster than anything inside the house.

## You keep the view

The fabric is an open weave, so this is not a wall. You see through it to the yard, the pool, or the water, and the air still moves. That is the difference between a patio shade and closing the space in, and it is why people who did not want to lose their view end up fitting them anyway.

## Manual or motorized

Rope-operated shades are simple and reliable. Motorized patio shades come down at the press of a button, which matters on a wide opening you adjust with the weather rather than once a season. On the motorized version, side channels cover the working parts, so you get the convenience without hardware on show. The breathable fabric wipes clean either way.

## Sizing and finish

Every opening is measured on site and the shade is made to it, because patio and lanai openings are almost never a standard size. Fabrics and colors are chosen to sit with the house rather than stand out from it.
