## The coast and everything behind it

Palm Beach County splits neatly at the interstate. East of it you have the barrier island, the Intracoastal, and the beachfront condo buildings, where light reflects off the water on top of direct sun and the view is the entire reason for the property. Solar screen shades are the usual starting point there, with a blackout layer behind them for bedrooms facing a sunrise. West of it are country club communities, planned developments, and a large number of 55+ communities, where the housing is more uniform, window sizes repeat, and whole-house orders are common.

## Shutters and the older architecture

The older parts of Boca Raton and Delray Beach have housing with real character: Mediterranean Revival, mid-century, and historic bungalows, with arched openings, deep reveals, and trim worth keeping. Plantation shutters are made to the opening rather than hung in front of it, which is why they finish those windows properly, and arches and specialty shapes are templated on site rather than squared off.

## Associations

A large share of the county is under an association, and many specify what a window reads like from the outside, usually a white or off-white face. That is simple to satisfy while the inside of the room still gets what you want, and we confirm the requirement before anything is ordered rather than after it is hung.
