## One county, several different briefs

Broward is not one kind of housing. The coastal cities have oceanfront condo towers and salt air, which decides material before it decides style. The western cities are master planned and newer, with two-story great rooms and long runs of rear glass facing an open horizon. The central cities are older, with mature tree canopy, smaller original openings, and very few standard sizes. And out at the edges there is still acreage, with large custom houses and tall glass. We measure every opening in all of them, because almost nothing here is a stock size.

## What that means in practice

Direct sun and humidity are the two constants. On anything taking hours of afternoon light, a solar screen keeps the view while taking the glare and heat down, and vinyl or composite holds its shape where wood would rather not. On the tall windows common in newer Broward construction, motorized shades stop being a luxury and start being the only sensible way to reach the glass. On a sliding door, which is the most common opening in the county, panel track shutters, verticals, and wide zebra shades all leave the doorway usable.
