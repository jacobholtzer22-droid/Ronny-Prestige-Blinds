## Not sure which one you want?

Most people arrive knowing the problem rather than the product: a room that is unusable at four in the afternoon, a slider nobody has ever covered properly, a bedroom that gets light at six in the morning, a window too high to reach. That is what the free in-home consultation is for. We look at the room, the exposure, and how you actually use it, then show you the two or three treatments that solve it, with samples, in the light they will sit in.

## How the range fits together

Shutters are built units fitted into the opening, which is why they read as part of the house and why they handle specialty shapes. Shades are fabric, which is where you get the range of opacity: sheer, light filtering, room darkening, blackout, and solar screen. Blinds are slats that tilt, horizontal or vertical, and they remain the most straightforward answer on a lot of ordinary windows. Drapery is the soft layer that finishes a room, and it works over any of the others.
