## How do I control them?

From a handheld remote, a wall switch, a phone app, or a smart home system with voice control. Most people end up using more than one: a wall switch by the door for the everyday, the app when they are out. If you already run a smart home setup, motorized shades can join it alongside the lights and the thermostat.

## Battery or hardwired?

Rechargeable battery motors need no wiring at all, which makes them the straightforward choice for a window that is awkward to get to or a room you would rather not open up. Hardwired motors run off the electrical system and never need charging. We go through which one suits each opening at the consultation, because it usually comes down to the window rather than a preference.

## Which windows are worth motorizing?

Tall windows and skylights you cannot reach without a ladder. Wide spans and sliding glass doors where a cord is a chore. Media rooms where you want the room dark in one movement. And any window you find yourself adjusting three or four times a day as the sun comes round. Motorized shades also remove the hanging cord entirely, which matters in a house with small children or pets.

## Which treatments can be motorized?

Roller shades, Roman shades, and zebra shades can all take a motor, and so can drapery. Our own team fits the motor, sets the open and closed limits, and programs the controls, so everything works the way you expect before we leave. If you are wondering whether shades you already own can be converted, call and describe them; that answer depends on the product.
