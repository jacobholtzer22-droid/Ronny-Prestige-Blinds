## Roller shades

One piece of fabric on a roller, raised by cord, chain, or motor. The simplest thing you can put on a window and often the right one. They come in vinyl, which wipes clean and stands up to humidity, and in fabric in three weights: light filtering to soften direct sun, room darkening for a cozier room, and blackout for total dark. A double roller setup puts a solar screen and a blackout shade on the same window, so you can cut glare through the day and close the room down at night. [Book a free consultation](/contact) and we will bring the fabric weights to compare.

## Solar screen shades

A woven open-weave fabric that blocks glare and heat while you can still see straight through it. This is the one for a wall of glass facing the afternoon sun, or a tall window where you want the view kept and the brightness taken off. It is the usual answer on a lanai. [Ask us to measure the opening](/contact) and we will show you how open or tight a weave suits your view.

## Sheer shades

A lightweight sheer fabric that diffuses incoming light rather than blocking it, so a room keeps its brightness without the hard edge of direct sun. They come in a range of colors and patterns and finish a room softly, which is why they turn up in living rooms and bedrooms more than anywhere else. [See them in your own room](/contact) and judge how they read against your wall color.

## Roman shades

Soft fabric shades that fold into pleats as they rise. The style is in how the fabric folds: flat for a clean stack, cascade for classic waterfall pleats, pleated for an accordion, aventura for a precise horizontal line, relaxed for a slight drape across the bottom, and tulip for gathered sides. Any of them can be motorized. [Ask us to bring samples](/contact) so you can see the styles in fabric rather than in a photograph.

## Outdoor patio shades

Screen enclosure shades for a patio or lanai, on a rope system or a motor. They cut UV, glare, and heat, hold off wind and rain across the opening, keep bugs out, and protect outdoor furniture from direct sun, which is most of what makes an outdoor room unusable here in August. Motorized versions hide the working parts in side channels so the look of the patio is kept. [Book a free consultation](/contact) and we will measure the patio.
