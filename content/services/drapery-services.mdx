{config.displayName} designs, makes, and installs custom drapery and curtains for homes and commercial properties in {config.primaryCity} and across South Florida, with the fabric, lining, hardware, and length all chosen to suit the room.

## How does a drapery project work?

It starts with a consultation at your home, where we look at the windows, talk through how the room is used, and work to the budget you have in mind. From there we put a design together, you choose the fabric from a wide range of textures and patterns, and we take precise measurements. Then the drapes are made, installed, and adjusted to hang properly, and we follow up afterwards to make sure you are happy with them.

## What are the options?

Drapes can be mounted on rod pockets, hooks, or tab tops, and hang with tie-backs or without. Lengths run from cafe style through to exact length, floor length, or puddle length pooling on the floor. Panels can be lined or unlined: a lining adds weight and body, blocks more light, and protects the face fabric from the sun, while unlined panels hang lighter and let more light through. We supply the hardware along with the drapery.

## Can drapery go over shades or blinds?

Yes, and it is a common pairing in a room that needs both. The shade does the light control and the drapery softens the window and finishes the room. The two need planning together so the mount points, the stack, and the clearance all work, which is why we look at them as one decision at the consultation rather than two.

## Can drapery be motorized?

Yes. Motorized drapery runs from a remote or a phone app and can be scheduled to open and close at set times, which is useful on a tall window or a run of panels across a wide opening. There are no cords, which makes it safer around small children and pets, and it can be added to a home automation system. See [motorized blinds and shades](/motorized-blinds) for more on how the controls work.
