{config.displayName} measures and installs blinds and window treatments throughout Parkland, Florida, a short drive north from our Coral Springs office.

## Parkland houses are built to a different scale

Parkland's zoning keeps lots large and density low, and the housing stock reflects it: newer gated subdivisions, generous frontages, equestrian trails rather than sidewalks in parts of the city, and very few apartment buildings. For window treatments that means more openings per house than average, more two-story entries, and a lot of rear glass facing the yard.

## The tall windows are the real decision

A two-story entry window, or a window above a staircase, is not somewhere anybody is going to stand with a wand twice a day. On Parkland jobs that is usually where the budget goes: a motorized blind or shade sized to the drop, with the open and closed limits set and the control programmed before we leave. Everything at normal height can stay manual without anybody minding.

## Ordering the whole house at once

Because Parkland houses are large, doing the house in one order rather than a room at a time is almost always the better buy. One measure, one delivery, one installation, and the same material and color carried through every room, which is what makes a big house read as finished rather than assembled over three years.

## Community review

Many Parkland communities review anything visible from the street, and a shutter or a blind on a front elevation is visible. We note what your community expects at the consultation, choose a frame and backing color that meets it, and say so in writing on the estimate.

## What it costs and how long it takes

We quote after measuring, never before. Same-day estimates are standard, same-day installation is available on in-stock products, and custom orders are built to your openings so those run longer. The consultation itself is free.
