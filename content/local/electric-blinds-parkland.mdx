## Why Parkland ends up motorized more than most

Ceiling heights. Parkland houses sit on large lots and tend to be built up as well as out, with two-story entries, tall great-room glass, and windows above staircases that nobody is reaching without a ladder. Those are exactly the openings a cord is useless on, and they are common enough here that motorized shades are a normal part of a Parkland quote rather than an upgrade we have to sell.

## Battery or hardwired in a Parkland house

Battery motors are rechargeable and need no wiring, which keeps the job clean in a finished house and is usually what we fit on a retrofit. Hardwired motors suit new construction or a remodel where the wiring is accessible, and they never need charging. On a tall window the practical question is how easy it will be to get back up there to recharge, and that is what decides it.

## Smart home and scheduling

Electric blinds run from a remote, a wall switch, or a phone app, and they can join a smart home system so voice control sits alongside your lights and thermostat. Scheduling is the feature people actually use: closing west-facing shades before the afternoon sun hits takes real load off the air conditioning, and it happens whether or not anybody is home to think about it.

## Safer around children and pets

There is no hanging cord to pull. In a family house that removes a hazard entirely, and it is one of the more common reasons Parkland customers move to motorized rather than replacing like with like.

## What happens on the day

We measure at the free consultation, order to your openings, then fit the motor, set the limits, pair the control, and connect it to your system. Same-day estimates are standard; custom orders are made to your windows, so we give you the installation date at the measure.
