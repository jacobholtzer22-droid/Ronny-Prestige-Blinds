## Shutters suit Boca Raton architecture

Boca Raton has a lot of Mediterranean Revival and mid-century housing, and both were designed around window proportions that shutters finish properly: tall, fairly narrow openings, arched tops, deep reveals. A framed shutter reads as part of that architecture in a way a soft treatment set in front of it never does. Inland, through the country club communities and the older single-family neighborhoods, shutters are the most requested treatment we fit here.

## Arches and specialty shapes

An arched or angled window is a normal shutter order, not a special one, because every shutter is made to its opening anyway. We template the shape on site at the consultation so the frame follows the curve rather than squaring it off. Specialty shapes are where a measured shutter most obviously beats anything off a shelf.

## Condos and association rules

A large share of Boca Raton is condo, much of it oceanfront, and associations commonly specify what a window reads like from the outside, usually a white or off-white face. That is easy to meet: the frame and louver color facing out can satisfy the rule while the room inside still gets the finish you want. We confirm the requirement before anything is ordered.

## Material near the water

On the barrier island and anywhere close to the ocean, vinyl and composite are the sensible call. They resist warping and fading and they wipe clean, which matters on a balcony slider that is opened every day in salt air. Wood stays the better-looking option further inland.

## Cost and timeline

Shutters are priced on the number of openings, the material, and the options, so we quote after the free in-home consultation rather than over the phone. Same-day estimates are standard, and the installation date is set once the measure is done.
