## What Coral Springs homes usually need

Coral Springs was largely built out from the 1970s onward as a planned city, and it shows in the windows. Most houses are single-family on a regular lot, with a sliding glass door onto the patio or pool deck, a cluster of bedroom windows at the back, and in the newer sections a two-story entry. That gives a fairly predictable order: horizontal blinds through the bedrooms and living space, something that stacks clear on the slider, and often a motorized unit for whatever is above the stairs.

## Humidity and sun are the two constraints

Wood blinds look the best and feel the best, and in a dry, shaded room they will last. In a bathroom, a kitchen, or a window that takes direct afternoon sun, vinyl and composite hold their shape where wood can warp, and they wipe clean rather than needing care. That is the trade-off we go through at the consultation, room by room, instead of applying one material to the whole house because it photographs well.

## Associations and what shows from the street

A lot of Coral Springs sits under a homeowners association, and many of them care about what the window reads like from outside even when they do not care what it looks like from inside. We ask about that before anything is ordered and pick a frame and backing that satisfies the rule, so nothing has to come back down after installation.
