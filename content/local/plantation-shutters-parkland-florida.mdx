## What suits a Parkland house

Parkland is mostly newer construction on large lots, in gated subdivisions built to a consistent standard. The architecture is cleaner-lined than the older housing further south, which is why hidden tilt shutters go into more Parkland homes than traditional ones: with no center rod running down the panel, the face stays flat and the louver spacing stays even, which suits a modern elevation.

## The tall windows

Two-story entries and windows above staircases are standard in these houses. Shutters work well up there precisely because they do not need daily handling: set the louvers once for the season and the window is solved. Where you do want daily control at height, the usual answer is shutters on the lower openings and a motorized shade above, and we plan the pair together so the two read as one decision.

## Material

Wood for the dry, shaded rooms where the grain is worth having. Vinyl and composite where there is humidity or hours of direct sun, because they resist warping and fading and they wipe clean. On a Parkland house that normally means a mix, and we work through it room by room at the consultation rather than applying one answer to the whole order.

## Community review

Most Parkland communities review what is visible from the street. In practice a white or off-white shutter is already what they expect, so shutters pass more easily than almost anything else, but we confirm the requirement before ordering and put it on the estimate.

## Measuring first

Parkland houses have very few standard openings, and a quote without a measure is a guess. The in-home consultation and the measure are both free, and you get the estimate and the schedule from that visit.
